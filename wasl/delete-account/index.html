Account and Data Deletion Request

If you created an account in the Wasl app and wish to delete your account and all associated data, you can do so in two ways:

1. In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

Open the Wasl app on your device > Go to Settings > Account > Tap Delete Account. This instantly and permanently deletes your account credentials, profile photo, and all cloud backup records.

2. Request Deletion Online

If you no longer have the app installed, please submit the form below. Your account and associated cloud data will be permanently deleted within 7 days of request verification.
